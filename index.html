About Alaa

Alaa Violin began his musical journey at the age of 12, starting with the violin. Over the years, music has grown into a lifelong passion and a way to connect with people through sound and performance.

He has collaborated with artists and musical groups from around the world, drawing inspiration from different musical backgrounds and styles. As a violinist and music producer, Alaa brings a personal, expressive approach to every musical project.

Based in the USA and available for events and collaborations nationwide.
